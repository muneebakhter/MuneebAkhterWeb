Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax‑deductible?

Yes. The ASPCA is a 501(c)(3) not‑for‑profit organization. Please retain your email receipt for your records.

Can I give monthly instead of one‑time?

Absolutely. Use the Monthly/One‑Time toggle above to select your preference. Monthly giving is one of the most efficient ways to help animals year‑round.

How will my gift be used?

Gifts support rescues, medical care, behavioral rehabilitation, placement, and advocacy to improve laws for animals.
